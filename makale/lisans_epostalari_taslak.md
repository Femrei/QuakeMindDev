## 1. TE23D (Ekkazan & Karslıgil, IEEE JSTARS 2025)

**Neden gerekli:** Makalede TE23D "CC BY-NC-ND" olarak anılıyor ama veri
kümesinin barındığı sayfada "tüm hakları saklıdır" ifadesi de geçiyor — bu
çelişki akademik kullanım/atıf öncesi netleştirilmeli.

**Konu:** Licensing clarification request — TE23D dataset (IEEE JSTARS 2025)

**Gövde:**

> Dear Dr. Ekkazan and Dr. Karslıgil,
>
> We are preparing a manuscript that compares building-damage segmentation
> results on the 6 February 2023 Kahramanmaraş earthquakes against your
> TE23D dataset and the results reported in your paper ("TE23D: A dataset
> for earthquake damage assessment and evaluation," IEEE JSTARS, 2025,
> doi:10.1109/JSTARS.2025.3526088).
>
> We noticed that the dataset's distribution page states a CC BY-NC-ND
> license, while another part of the same page states "all rights
> reserved." Could you please confirm the exact license terms under which
> TE23D may be used and cited for academic, non-commercial research
> (specifically, whether derivative comparison tables/figures referencing
> your reported metrics are permitted)?
>
> Thank you very much for your time, and congratulations on the dataset
> and paper.
>
> Best regards,
> [İsim] — [KTO Karatay Üniversitesi]

## 2. TUE-CD (Liu vd., IEEE TGRS 2024)

**Neden gerekli:** README'de lisans hiç belirtilmemiş.

**Konu:** License information request — TUE-CD dataset (IEEE TGRS 2024)

**Gövde:**

> Dear authors,
>
> We are preparing a manuscript on satellite-imagery damage segmentation
> for the 6 February 2023 Kahramanmaraş earthquakes and would like to use
> the TUE-CD dataset ("Building change detection in earthquake: A
> multiscale interaction network with offset calibration and a dataset,"
> IEEE TGRS, 2024, doi:10.1109/TGRS.2024.3437470) as an external test set.
>
> We could not find explicit licensing terms in the dataset's repository/
> README. Could you kindly let us know under what license TUE-CD is
> released, and whether it may be used for academic, non-commercial
> research and benchmarking purposes?
>
> Thank you for making this dataset available, and best regards,
> [İsim] — [KTO Karatay Üniversitesi]
